          Privacy & data

          This game uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how it is used — only if you accept. Analytics is off by default and never loads until you click Accept.

          What is collected (only after you accept)

          • Page views, session and engagement, approximate (city-level) location with your IP anonymised.
          • Device, browser and language.
          • Referrer and utm_* campaign tags from the link you arrived on.
          • Gameplay events (a game started, a game ended, a score shared, a leaderboard view, etc.).

          What is never collected

          • No name, email or other personal information.
          • Never your 6-digit session code.
          • No precise location.

          Cookies

          If you accept, GA4 sets first-party cookies _ga and _ga_G-0DFSB38H21 to count visits and sessions (they expire after up to ~2 years). Declining sets no analytics cookies.

          Your choices

          • Click Decline to keep analytics off.
          • If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, analytics stays off automatically and no banner is shown.
          • Clear this site's data to reset your choice and withdraw consent.
          • See how Google uses data.

          Playing the game (pairing a phone, the joystick stream, the leaderboard) is functional data you opt into by playing — it is separate from analytics and is not affected by this choice.

          We use Google Analytics to understand how the game is used. No personal data or your session code is collected.
